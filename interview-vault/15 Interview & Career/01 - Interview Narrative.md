## Structure (recommended)

Present yourself in four blocks:

1. **Education** — foundation (engineering → ML MSc → PhD)
2. **Industry** — applied ML, production systems, agents
3. **Research** — publications, novel methods, domain impact
4. **Why this role** — link their stack to your experience

**Tip:** Not too nervous — interview is a **conversation**, not an exam. Use this vault for technical depth when they drill down.
