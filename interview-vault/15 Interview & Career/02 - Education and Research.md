## Research highlights

### Soundscape ecology (2018–2020)

**Problem:** Heterogeneity of soundscapes → landscape transformation.  
**Method:** Unsupervised GMM, acoustic clusters, functional characteristics.  
**Challenge:** Choosing number of clusters — high uncertainty.

### Cluster validity index (2021–2024)

**Problem:** Gap in internal validity for ecoacoustic clustering.  
**Contribution:** Uncertainty clustering validity using Fréchet distance between distributions.  
**Collaboration:** University of Antioquia + MIA, La Rochelle University.

**Publication:** Rendon et al. (2023), *Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence*.

### Landscape acoustic heterogeneity (2025)

**Idea:** Ecosystems "speak" through acoustic patterns — unsupervised mapping of landscape heterogeneity.  
**Publication:** Rendon et al. (2025), *Environmental Modelling & Software*.

### Ongoing — acoustic "language" & GCN

Patterns as structured communication; taxonomy of relationships; **Graph Convolutional Network** for landscape transformation (knowledge-graph perspective).

**KB paper in process:** landscape ecology terminology challenges.
