# Central Limit Theorem

[[MOC - Statistics & Probability|← Statistics]]

The **CLT**: under mild conditions, the distribution of the sample mean (normalized) approaches a **normal distribution** as \(n\) grows — even if the original data are not normal.

**Law of large numbers:** the sample average converges to the true mean as sample size increases.

**Why it matters in interviews:** justifies normal approximations, confidence intervals, and many hypothesis tests.
