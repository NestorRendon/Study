# PCA & Factor Analysis

[[MOC - Statistics & Probability|← Statistics]]

**ANOVA:** compare means across **3+ groups** (see [[ANOVA Analysis of Variance]]).

**PCA:** linear dimensionality reduction — orthogonal directions of maximum variance.

**Factor analysis:** model observed variables as linear functions of fewer **latent factors**; aims to reproduce the correlation structure.

**Population tests:** compare samples to a reference or across populations (t-tests, ANOVA, non-parametric alternatives when assumptions fail).
